# Neon Tris - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026
App: Neon Tris: Tic Tac Toe Glow (Android) - package `com.appvitastudio.neontris`
Data controller: AppVita Studio - contact: appvitastudio@proton.me

**Neon Tris** ("the game") is a Tic Tac Toe game. This policy explains what data the game handles.

## Data the game stores on your device
Your progress points, level, unlocked and equipped neon effects, statistics and settings (music, sounds,
vibration, reduced effects, language) are saved **only on your device**. They are never sent to us.
Uninstalling the game deletes them.

The game works offline. It has no account, no login, no chat, no online multiplayer, and it does not ask for
access to contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone. The 2-player mode is played on the same device.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The game is free and shows ads through **Google AdMob** (never during a game and never on the grid). To show and
measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location),
device and app information, and interactions with ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and
Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown (Google User Messaging Platform), and you
can change your choice at any time from **Settings > Privacy choices** in the game.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children
The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address.

AppVita Studio - appvitastudio@proton.me

# Neon Tris - Informativa sulla privacy (Italiano)

**Neon Tris** salva progressi, livello, effetti neon sbloccati ed equipaggiati, statistiche e impostazioni
**solo sul tuo dispositivo**: non ci vengono mai inviati e vengono cancellati disinstallando il gioco. Il gioco
funziona offline, senza account, senza chat e senza multiplayer online; non chiede accesso a contatti, foto,
posizione, fotocamera o microfono.

Il gioco è gratuito e mostra pubblicità tramite **Google AdMob** (mai durante una partita e mai sulla griglia).
Google può trattare dati come l'ID pubblicità, l'indirizzo IP (posizione approssimativa), informazioni sul
dispositivo e sulle interazioni con gli annunci. Nello Spazio economico europeo, nel Regno Unito e in Svizzera
viene chiesto il consenso prima degli annunci personalizzati e la scelta si può cambiare in qualsiasi momento da
**Impostazioni > Scelte privacy**. Contatto: appvitastudio@proton.me
